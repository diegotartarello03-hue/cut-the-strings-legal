Cut the Strings - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Who we are. Cut the Strings is a small animated video channel (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok: @cuthestrings). This policy covers the internal tool we use to upload our own videos to our own accounts.

What the tool does. It sends videos that we created to the Cut the Strings accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, and reads the public view and like counts of those same videos. It is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to other people.

What data we handle. Only the access tokens of the channel owner's own accounts, and the public statistics of our own videos. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data of other TikTok, Instagram or YouTube users.

Where data is stored. On the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to third parties other than the platforms themselves, in order to publish and read our own videos.

Your rights. If you have any question or want something removed, contact us by direct message on TikTok or Instagram @cuthestrings.

Changes. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
